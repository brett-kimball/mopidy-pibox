# pibox Quick Start Guide

## Getting Started

1. **Connect to the WiFi** network provided by your host
2. **Scan the QR code** displayed on the TV screen to open the music app on your phone

---

## Adding Music

Tap the **search icon** in the top right corner to find music.

- **Search** — Type an artist or song name
- **Browse** — Scroll through the artist list alphabetically

Tap any song to add it to the queue. Your selection will play in order after the current queue.

---

## Managing the Queue

- **Vote to skip** — Tap the thumbs-down on a song to vote for it to be skipped. If enough people vote, the song will be removed from the queue.
- **Remove your songs** — You can remove songs you added by tapping on them in the queue.

---

## Tips

- There may be a limit on how many songs you can queue at once
- The queue is shared with everyone — be a good DJ!
- Check the TV screen to see what's playing now

---

*Enjoy the music!*
